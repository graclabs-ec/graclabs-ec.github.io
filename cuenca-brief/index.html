Notes

The Cuenca Brief is a short, friendly news briefing for English speakers living in and around Cuenca, Ecuador: news from Cuenca and Azuay, the big stories from across Ecuador and the Andes, practical notices you can use, opportunities, science and technology, culture, events and a little sports.

When new episodes arrive (Cuenca time, UTC−5):
Sundays around 5:00 am: the week that was and the week ahead (up to 15 minutes)
Tuesdays around 4:00 pm: the latest news (up to 10 minutes)
Thursdays around 11:00 am: a quick catch-up and your weekend preview (up to 10 minutes)
Fridays around 5:00 am: a short weekend update (up to 5 minutes)

First regular episode: Tuesday, November 17, 2026, around 4:00 pm. Sources for every story are listed in each episode's notes.
