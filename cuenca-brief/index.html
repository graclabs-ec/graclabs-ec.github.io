Welcome to The Cuenca Brief

2026-11-15 · 1 min

Notes

Meet The Cuenca Brief, a short, friendly news briefing for English speakers living in and around Cuenca, Ecuador: news from Cuenca and Azuay, the big stories from across Ecuador and the Andes, practical notices you can use, opportunities, culture and events.

New episodes: early Sunday morning (the week that was and the week ahead), Tuesday afternoon, Thursday late morning (your weekend preview) and early Friday morning (a quick weekend update). Sources for every story are listed in each episode's notes.
